## The game is called Outlast
It is a turn based combat game.
The player's goal is to explore and fight enemies. 
The core loop is exploring and fighting enemies and winning .
An important rule or mechanic of it is the fighting part and making sure you don't die .
The first playable version includes some of the graphic art, the GUI, some of the map health bar, and stamina bar some examples of enemies. Some features we are saving for later are the abilities and making sure they work, weapons that go with the abilities the character the player will play , and better and or more enemies that will appear around the map. The end goal is exploring all around the map/world and fighting new and more enemies and winning/ defeating the enemies and fighting  against the enemies and upgrading/ making your character look cool and do more damage against higher level enemies. The Player can also attack defend or use spells to survive or defeat the enemy
